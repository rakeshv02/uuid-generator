Frequently Asked Questions

What is a UUID?
A UUID (Universally Unique Identifier) is a 128-bit number used to uniquely identify information. UUID v4 is randomly generated.
Is this secure?
Yes — uses crypto.randomUUID() or crypto.getRandomValues() for true cryptographic randomness.
